Physical NFC experience

One personalized NFC invitation or keepsake can open the event website. Optional table stands link to the photo gallery. After the event, the same NFC can point to the memories album while the subscription remains active.

A production version requires private photo storage, a database, secure couple accounts, invitation tokens, payment/subscription management, and deletion/export workflows. None of those services are activated in this preview.
